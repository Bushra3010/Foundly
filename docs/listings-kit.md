## Names and descriptions

- **Tagline (60 characters max):** Get your business recommended by ChatGPT and AI search
- **Short description (about 160 characters):** Foundly is an SEO, AEO and GEO agency in Pune that helps businesses get recommended by ChatGPT, Gemini, Perplexity and Claude.
- **Long description:**

  Foundly is an SEO, AEO and GEO agency and AI search visibility platform based in Pune, India, working with businesses across India and worldwide.

  More customers now ask ChatGPT, Gemini, Perplexity and Claude who to call instead of scrolling search results. Foundly checks whether those assistants name your business for the questions your customers ask, shows which competitors they recommend instead and which sources they cite, and works through the fixes with you.

  Every plan includes basic GEO (generative engine optimisation), AEO (answer engine optimisation) foundations such as structured data and quotable FAQs, local SEO foundations, a ChatGPT Ads service and a weekly report. Every AI answer is saved as evidence, so results can be checked rather than taken on trust. Plans start at ₹15,000 a month.
