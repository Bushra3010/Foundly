We research with institutions, not about them

Collaboration takes several forms — joint studies, field access, methodological review, data sharing and co-publication. Named partnerships are listed once a collaboration agreement is signed and the partner has approved publication.

Universities

Joint studies, doctoral supervision links, methodological peer review and co-authored publications with departments of law, education, public policy and social sciences.

Government Agencies

Evidence support for education departments, child protection bodies and regulatory authorities, including framework alignment and implementation review.

Think Tanks

Shared policy roundtables, comparative analysis and joint briefs on constitutional literacy, governance and access to justice.

NGOs & Civil Society

Field access, community-level data collection and practitioner insight from organisations working directly with the populations a study concerns.

Corporate & CSR

Funded research programmes and workplace studies conducted under published independence conditions, with findings released whatever they show.
