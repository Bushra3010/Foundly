Working at SWAMITRA

Small team, long horizon, unglamorous work that matters.

Current openings

There are no vacancies published at this moment. Openings are advertised on this page as they arise, with the role, location, term and the essential requirements stated in full — we do not run standing adverts for roles that do not exist.

Speculative applications are welcome and are genuinely read. Send your CV with a short note naming the function you are interested in — programme delivery, research, curriculum, communications, operations or finance — and what you would want to work on. We keep applications on file for twelve months and contact people when a matching role opens.

What we look for

  • Precision — this is work where a wrong statement of the law causes harm
  • Willingness to be measured, including when the numbers are poor
  • Ability to explain a legal idea to someone who has never studied one
  • Comfort working in more than one Indian language
  • Patience with institutions that move slowly

Recruitment process

ApplicationCV and a short note on the role. Every application is acknowledged.
ScreeningShortlisting against the published requirements, sometimes with a short written task.
InterviewOne or two conversations covering subject knowledge, judgement and working style.
Reference checkTwo references, contacted only with your consent, plus screening where the role is child-facing.
OfferA written offer stating role, term, remuneration and reporting line before you are asked to accept.

Equal opportunity statement

SWAMITRA Foundation selects and promotes people on the basis of merit and the requirements of the role, and on nothing else. We do not discriminate on grounds of religion, race, caste, sex, gender identity, sexual orientation, place of birth, language, descent, marital status, pregnancy, age, disability or economic background, whether in recruitment, remuneration, training, promotion or termination.

We ask about a candidate's requirements rather than assuming them, and we make reasonable adjustments to the application process, the interview and the workplace so that a disabled candidate is assessed on the work and not on the arrangements. Our selection panels record the reason for every shortlisting decision, so that reasoning can be reviewed.

We hold ourselves to the same standards we ask of the institutions we train. Anyone who believes a recruitment decision at the Foundation was made on a prohibited ground may raise it under our Whistleblower Policy, and it will be examined.
