The people accountable for the Foundation's work

SWAMITRA is governed by a Board of Directors and run day to day by an executive team. The Board sets strategy, approves budgets and holds the executive to account; the executive delivers programmes within the mandate the Board sets.

Named profiles, photographs and biographies are published on this page as appointments are formally confirmed and the corresponding statutory filings are complete. Until then we list the roles rather than attribute work to individuals who have not yet taken office.

Founder

FD Founder & Managing Director Sets the founding mandate, chairs the Objects and Mission review, and is accountable to the Board for the Foundation's direction. Profile to be published.

Board of Directors

The Board meets at least quarterly. It approves the annual plan and budget, receives audit and compliance reports, and reviews every material risk and conflict of interest disclosure.
