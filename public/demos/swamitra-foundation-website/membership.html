Join the network

Membership connects individuals and institutions working on the same problems in different places.

Individual

Individual Membership

For teachers, lawyers, HR professionals, researchers, students and citizens who want to stay close to this work and use it in their own setting.

  • Early access to frameworks, toolkits and research before public release
  • Discounted registration on paid certification programmes
  • Invitations to conclaves, roundtables and member-only briefings
  • The members' newsletter with method notes, not just announcements
  • The right to propose research questions and comment on draft standards
  • Listing in the member directory, if you choose to be listed
Institutional

Institutional Membership

For schools, colleges, universities, companies, NGOs and public bodies implementing our frameworks across a whole institution.

  • Institution-wide access to frameworks, templates and audit tools
  • Priority scheduling for training cohorts and safety audits
  • Recognition as a Participating Institution in the national network
  • An annual implementation review with written recommendations
  • Nomination rights to the master trainer programme
  • A named relationship contact at the Foundation

What membership costs

Membership categories and any associated fee will be published here once the Foundation's membership structure is formally approved by its governing body. Until then, registration below records your interest and we will write to you when the structure is live, with the terms in full and no obligation to proceed.

Members are expected to uphold the Code of Ethics and to use Foundation material with attribution. Membership does not confer any authority to speak on behalf of the Foundation, to certify third parties, or to describe a commercial service as SWAMITRA-approved.

Membership registration

Registering interest creates no obligation and no charge. Details are handled in line with our Privacy Policy.
