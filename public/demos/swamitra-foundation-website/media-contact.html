Working on a story?

Tell us your outlet, your subject and your deadline. We will tell you honestly whether we can help, and by when.

  • Interviews with programme leads on legal education, school safety, workplace dignity and child protection
  • Data and methodology notes behind any published figure
  • Review copies of frameworks, standards and research reports
  • Fact-checking support on constitutional and legal literacy claims

We do not offer comment on individual cases, ongoing inquiries or matters before a court or statutory authority. Where a question falls outside our competence, we will say so and, where we can, point you to someone better placed to answer it.

If you are working to a fixed publication time, tell us the date and we will prioritise accordingly.

Media enquiries are acknowledged within one working day. Details you send are used only to respond to your enquiry — see our Privacy Policy.
