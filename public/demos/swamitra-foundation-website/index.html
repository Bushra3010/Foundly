Who We Are

A national institution built around one conviction — that knowing the law is a form of freedom.

SWAMITRA Foundation is a not-for-profit organisation committed to building a legally aware, constitutionally informed, safe and responsible society. We work through legal education, research, capacity building and institutional partnerships to strengthen individuals and institutions across India.
