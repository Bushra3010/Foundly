Office Hours

When we are available

Working days

Monday to Friday. The office is closed on Saturdays, Sundays and gazetted public holidays.

Training delivery and community camps frequently run on weekends; those are scheduled directly with the host institution and are not affected by office hours.

Working hours

Email is monitored through the working day. Messages received after hours or over a weekend are picked up on the next working morning.

Holiday notice

The Foundation observes gazetted public holidays. Response times extend by the length of any holiday period, and enquiries received during a closure are answered in the order they arrived.

Closures of more than two consecutive working days are announced in advance on the News page.

Emergency & Compliance

Compliance contacts

These roles exist inside the Foundation and cover conduct by our own people, our trainers, volunteers and partners in the course of our work.

This website is not an emergency service. If a child or an adult is in immediate danger, do not use this form. Contact the police, the national emergency number 112, the childline service 1098, or the women's helpline 181, and where the law requires it, report the matter to the Child Welfare Committee or the Special Juvenile Police Unit without delay. A message sent here may not be read until the next working day, and that delay could matter.

How a concern is handled

Every concern is acknowledged, recorded and assessed. The identity of the person raising it is protected to the fullest extent possible, and retaliation against anyone who raises a concern in good faith is itself a disciplinary offence under our Whistleblower Policy.

Where a matter falls to a statutory authority, we report it as the law requires and do not conduct our own investigation first.

A note on names

We publish roles rather than individuals. The person holding a role changes; the obligation does not. Naming the role means a concern reaches whoever currently holds it, including during a handover, and it protects the privacy of the officers concerned.

When you write, mark your message for the role. It will be routed to that officer and will not be read by anyone else in the Foundation.
