📋 Overview

A Transparent, Merit-Based Process

Himalaya Valley University (HVU) offers a streamlined and transparent admission process to ensure that prospective students can conveniently apply and gain admission to their preferred programs. The university is committed to selecting and enrolling students who exhibit academic excellence, personal integrity, and a strong desire for holistic development.

Our admission procedure is thoughtfully designed to be student-friendly, guiding applicants step-by-step from initial inquiry to final enrollment, while maintaining fairness, transparency, and merit-based selection at its core.

📩 Step-by-Step Online Admission Process

1️⃣

Online Registration

Visit the official website and fill out the online registration form.

2️⃣

Application Submission

Complete the application form with personal, academic, and program-related details. Upload required documents.

3️⃣

Document Verification

Submitted documents are verified for eligibility and authenticity.

4️⃣

Entrance Exam / Merit Evaluation

Depending on the program, admission is either entrance-based or merit-based.

5️⃣

Admission Letter

Selected candidates receive an official Admission Letter via email or portal.

6️⃣

Fee Payment

Pay the prescribed admission fee through online/offline mode.

7️⃣

Final Enrollment

After payment confirmation, you are officially enrolled in the program.

📝 Step-by-Step Offline Admission Process

  1. 1Visit the university campus or authorized admission centers.
  2. 2Collect the printed admission form from the Admission Office.
  3. 3Fill the form manually with blue/black pen.
  4. 4Attach photocopies of required documents.
  5. 5Submit the completed form at the Admission Office with the application fee (cash/DD).
  6. 6Collect the receipt and wait for further communication via SMS/email.
🛑 Important: Always keep a photocopy of your filled form and payment receipt.

📄 Required Documents

  • ✔️Passport-size photograph
  • ✔️10th & 12th mark sheets and certificates (for UG programs)
  • ✔️Graduation mark sheets (for PG programs)
  • ✔️Identity proof (Aadhaar/PAN/Passport)
  • ✔️Transfer/Migration Certificate
  • ✔️Category certificate (if applicable)
  • ✔️Entrance exam scorecard (if applicable)

✅ Eligibility Criteria

💳 Payment Procedure

Himalaya Valley University (HVU) offers both online and offline modes of fee payment to ensure convenience and accessibility for all students.

🌐

Online Payment Methods

Net Banking, Credit/Debit Card, UPI (Google Pay, PhonePe, Paytm, etc.), and Online Wallets. Log in to the student portal, navigate to the Fee Payment section, choose your mode, complete the transaction, and download the fee receipt.

🏦

Offline Payment Options

Demand Draft (DD) in favor of Himalaya Valley University, payable at Sikkim; cash payment at the admission/finance office; or Bank Transfer (NEFT/RTGS) to the official university account.

All payments must be made before the specified deadline to confirm admission or semester enrollment. Ensure to collect the payment receipt or acknowledgment slip after payment.

🎓 Admission Modes & Scholarships

🥇

Merit-Based Admission

Admission granted on the basis of academic performance and merit in qualifying examinations.

🧮

Entrance Exam-Based Admission

Admission based on performance in the prescribed entrance examination for relevant programs.

🎯

Direct &amp; Lateral Entry

Direct admission under management quota (if applicable) and Lateral Entry for eligible diploma holders or transfer students.

🏅

Scholarships

HVU offers merit-based, need-based, and category-specific scholarships. Students can apply during the admission process with relevant documents.
