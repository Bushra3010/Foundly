💰 Policy Overview

Fee Refund Policy

Sengol International University follows the refund norms prescribed by the UGC to ensure fairness and transparency for students who choose to withdraw from a program after admission. Refunds are calculated based on the date of cancellation relative to the last date of admission.

Students are encouraged to read this policy carefully before remitting fees. All refund requests are processed in accordance with the slabs detailed below.

📜

UGC Compliant

Our refund structure adheres to the latest University Grants Commission guidelines on fee refunds.

⚖️

Fair & Transparent

Refund amounts are calculated objectively based on the date of withdrawal.

⏱️

Timely Processing

Approved refunds are credited to the original payment source within 30 working days.

Refund Schedule

Time of CancellationRefund Amount
15 days or more before the last date of admission100% (less ₹1,000 processing charge)
Less than 15 days before the last date of admission90% of the fee paid
15 days or less after the last date of admission80% of the fee paid
30 days or less after the last date of admission50% of the fee paid
More than 30 days after the last date of admissionNo refund

Refund Procedure

1️⃣

Step 1

Submit a written refund request to the Admissions Office along with the original fee receipt.

2️⃣

Step 2

Attach supporting documents and a copy of the withdrawal/cancellation letter.

3️⃣

Step 3

The request is verified and forwarded to the Accounts Department for processing.

4️⃣

Step 4

Approved refunds are credited to the original payment source within 30 working days.

Non-Refundable Charges

  • •Application and registration fees
  • •Admission processing charges
  • •Examination and re-evaluation fees
  • •Hostel caution deposit (adjusted against dues, if any)

Have Questions About Refunds?

For any clarification regarding the refund policy or to initiate a refund request, please reach out to our Admissions Office. Refunds are processed strictly as per UGC norms.
