💰 Financial Support

Scholarships and Financial Aid

At Sengol International University, we are committed to supporting bright and talented students through a range of scholarships and financial aid programs. Every year, the college offers substantial financial assistance to deserving undergraduate and postgraduate students to help them achieve their academic goals.

🏆

Merit-Based Scholarships

Up to 100% tuition fee waiver for exceptional academic performers with outstanding achievements in their previous studies.

🤝

Need-Based Financial Aid

Comprehensive financial support packages including tuition assistance, accommodation grants, and study material allowances.

🌍

International Student Scholarships

Special scholarships for international students with cultural diversity initiatives and global leadership development programs.
