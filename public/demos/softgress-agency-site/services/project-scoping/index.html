Project Scoping & Proposal: frequently asked questions

What is included in Project Scoping & Proposal from Softgress?

We turn your brief into a scoped proposal with deliverables, milestones, assumptions and a quote. A request is not a contract until both sides sign. Typical deliverables are scope of work, milestones, assumptions & exclusions and quote, agreed in a written proposal before work starts.

How does the Project Scoping & Proposal process work?

We follow 5 steps: brief review, clarifications, proposal, revisions and sign-off. You get regular check-ins and reviews at each stage, run by our Client Strategy & Project Gallery team.

What do you need from me to get started?

To scope Project Scoping & Proposal accurately we ask for: project brief, decision makers, budget range and timeline. If you don't have everything yet, a short discovery call is enough to begin.

How much does Project Scoping & Proposal cost?

Project Scoping & Proposal is quoted per project, because cost depends on scope, integrations, content readiness and timeline. Requesting a quote or a consultation is free, and you get a written quote before any work begins.

Do you offer Project Scoping & Proposal outside Pune?

Yes. Our team works from Pimpri-Chinchwad, Pune and delivers Project Scoping & Proposal for clients in Mumbai, Bangalore, across India and internationally, with remote collaboration and in-person meetings by arrangement.
