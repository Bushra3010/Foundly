Frequently asked questions

What does Softgress do?

Softgress is a software development and digital marketing company based in Pimpri-Chinchwad, Pune. Five teams under one roof build websites, mobile apps, SaaS and CRM systems, run SEO and Google and Meta ads, create brand and video content, and set up AI chatbots and WhatsApp automation.

Where is Softgress located?

Our office is at Akurdi Chowk, Shubhashri Residency, Ganga Nagar, Akurdi, Pimpri-Chinchwad, Maharashtra 411035, in the Pune metropolitan region. You can call or WhatsApp us on +91 74472 07700.

Do you work with clients outside Pune?

Yes. We work with businesses in Mumbai, Bangalore and across India, and with international clients in the US, UK, UAE, Canada and Australia. Projects run remotely with video calls, shared project boards and regular demos, and in-person meetings can be arranged.

Why choose one agency for software and marketing?

When the same team plans your website, campaigns, content and automation, every piece works together and nobody loses context in handovers. You get one written brief, one point of contact and one team accountable for the result.

How much does a website, app or marketing project cost?

Every project is quoted after a short scoping call, because cost depends on features, integrations, content readiness and timeline. Requesting a quote or consultation is free and does not commit you to anything. Ad spend is paid directly to the ad platform.

How do I start a project with Softgress?

Send an enquiry on the website, call or WhatsApp +91 74472 07700. We hold a discovery conversation, send a written proposal with scope, milestones and a quote, then deliver in phases with regular check-ins, launch and ongoing support.
