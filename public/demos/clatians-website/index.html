Everything about CLAT preparation at CLATians.

CLAT 2026 is a 2-hour Computer Based Test (CBT) with 120 questions across five sections: English Language (22–26 Qs), Current Affairs & GK (28–32 Qs), Legal Reasoning (28–32 Qs), Logical Reasoning (22–26 Qs), and Quantitative Techniques (10–14 Qs). Each correct answer awards 1 mark; -0.25 for every wrong answer.
