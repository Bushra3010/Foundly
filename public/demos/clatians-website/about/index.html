Our Story

From a Classroom in Patna
to India's #1 CLAT Institute

Founded in 2012 by NLU alumni who saw one truth: students from Bihar were just as talented — but lacked the platform to compete nationally.

We started with 15 students, one classroom, and one conviction — talent is equally distributed. Opportunity is not.

Twelve years, 5000+ NLU selections and 1.25 lakh students later — that belief still drives every class we teach.
