Applicable requirements may include obtaining verifiable parental consent before processing personal data of a child and restrictions concerning tracking, behavioural monitoring and targeted advertising directed at children.

Accordingly, where legally required, LexPrep Academy will seek appropriate parental or lawful-guardian consent before processing a child's personal data.

Parents or lawful guardians may contact LexPrep Academy regarding the personal information of a child using the contact details provided in this Privacy Policy.
