Overview

LSAT India is the Law School Admission Test adapted for India, conducted by LSAC (Law School Admission Council) in partnership with Pearson VUE. It is accepted by 85+ private law universities in India including Jindal Global Law School (JGLS), UPES, Alliance University, and other prestigious institutions. LSAT India tests analytical reasoning, logical reasoning, and reading comprehension — skills critical for success in law school. It is conducted in online remote proctored mode.

✅

Eligibility Criteria

1Completed Class 12 for 5-year integrated BA LLB program
2Completed graduation for 3-year LLB program

How to Apply

1

Register on the official LSAC India website (lsac.org/india) with your email ID and create your account

2

Fill in your personal details, academic background, and select your preferred test date and city

3

Upload a recent passport-size photograph as per the specifications on the portal

4

Pay the registration fee online (₹3,999 standard) via debit card, credit card, or net banking

5

Download your LSAT India admission ticket and keep it ready for exam day along with a valid photo ID

Frequently Asked Questions

QWhat is LSAT India?+
LSAT India is the Law School Admission Test adapted for India by LSAC, accepted by 85+ private law universities including Jindal Global Law School.
QIs there negative marking in LSAT India?+
No, there is no negative marking in LSAT India. You should attempt all questions.
QHow is LSAT India different from CLAT?+
LSAT India does not test legal knowledge — it tests pure reasoning and reading skills. It is accepted by private universities, while CLAT is for NLUs.
QDoes CLATians prepare students for LSAT India?+
Yes! CLATians covers LSAT India as part of the OLET program with specialized analytical and logical reasoning training.
