Overview

CUET-LAW (Common University Entrance Test for Law) is conducted by NTA for admission to law programs at central universities, state universities, and private universities across India. It opens doors to prestigious institutions like BHU (Banaras Hindu University), DU (Delhi University), JNU, Jamia Millia Islamia, and 200+ other universities. CUET-LAW offers UG and PG admission options with a comprehensive test covering legal studies, general knowledge, and language proficiency.

✅

Eligibility Criteria

1Completed or appearing in Class 12 for UG programs
2Minimum 50% marks for General category (varies by university)
3Minimum 45% marks for OBC category
4Minimum 40% marks for SC/ST/PWD category
5Completed graduation/LLB for PG programs

How to Apply

1

Register on the official CUET portal (cuet.samarth.ac.in) with email and phone number

2

Fill in your personal details and select universities and courses (including law programmes) you wish to apply for

3

Choose your preferred test language and upload photograph and signature as per specifications

4

Pay the application fee online — fee varies by number of subjects and category

5

Download and print the CUET application form confirmation and keep it safe

Frequently Asked Questions

QWhat is CUET-LAW?+
CUET-LAW is the Common University Entrance Test for Law conducted by NTA for admission to law programs at central, state, and private universities accepting CUET scores.
QWhich universities accept CUET-LAW scores?+
Over 200 universities accept CUET scores including BHU, DU, JNU, Jamia Millia Islamia, and many state universities. Each university sets its own cutoff.
QIs CUET-LAW different from CLAT?+
Yes. CUET-LAW is for central and state universities (not NLUs), has a different format including a Legal Studies domain paper, and is conducted by NTA.
