Privacy Policy

Who we are

Skyline Homes is a real estate development company with its registered office at Skyline House, Level 4, 12 Lakeview Road, Vastrapur, Ahmedabad, Gujarat 380015. This policy explains what personal information we collect through this website and what we do with it.

What we collect

We collect only what you give us through a form on this site: your name, mobile number, email address, the project you are interested in, your requirement, a preferred site visit date and time, and any message you write. We do not collect financial information through this website, and we never ask for card, bank or identity document details online.

How we use it

Your details are used to respond to your enquiry, confirm a site visit, share a brochure, floor plan or cost sheet, and to keep you updated on the specific project you asked about. Enquiries are handled by our own in-house sales and project teams.

What we do not do

We do not sell, rent or trade your personal information. We do not pass your details to brokers, channel partners or third-party listing platforms. We do not send unrelated marketing on behalf of other companies.

Sharing

We share information only where it is necessary to serve you: for example, with the site engineering team hosting your visit: or where we are required to by law, a court, or a regulatory authority such as the Real Estate Regulatory Authority.

Storage on your device

This website stores your enquiry reference and site preferences in your own browser using local storage. That data stays on your device. Clearing your browser storage removes it.

Third-party services

Pages that show a location embed a Google Map. When a map loads, Google may set its own cookies and receive your IP address under its own privacy policy. Fonts are loaded from Google Fonts on the same basis.

How long we keep it

Enquiry records are retained for as long as they are commercially relevant, and thereafter for the period required under applicable law and RERA record-keeping obligations.

Your rights

You may ask us at any time for a copy of the information we hold about you, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete it and stop contacting you. Write to hello@skylinehomes.example.com and we will act on the request within a reasonable period.

Changes

We may update this policy as our practices or the law change. The version published on this page is the version that applies.

Questions about this policy?

Write to us and we will respond within one working day.
