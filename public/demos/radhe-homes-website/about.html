About Skyline Homes

We develop. We don’t broker, list or resell.

Skyline Homes is a real estate development company focused on creating quality residential and commercial spaces.

Every project on this website is our own: our land, our design team, our contractors, our site engineers and our handover. That single fact changes everything about how a buyer is treated: there is no third party to point at when something slips, and no commission structure quietly shaping what you are shown.

We plan with strong attention to construction quality, location and long-term value, combining modern infrastructure with practical design and a reliable customer experience. Since 2009 that approach has produced eight developments across Ahmedabad and homes for more than six hundred families.

We stay deliberately small in number of concurrent projects. It is the only way we know to keep a promised possession date honest.

Handover & Service

Land is acquired with clear title and independently vetted before a single drawing is made. Every project is registered with GujRERA before a rupee is collected, and the approved plan is what gets built: no silent substitutions of specification.

During construction, buyers receive a dated progress report every month, including photographs. After handover, a dedicated service desk stays with the project for the full defect-liability period rather than closing the file on possession day.
