Zavtoo Privacy Policy

How Zavtoo Paani Filter Pvt Ltd handles information in the Zavtoo app and on zavtoo.in, including customer, technician and dealer accounts.

  • Applies to Zavtoo Android app and website

1Who we are

Zavtoo Paani Filter Pvt Ltd operates Zavtoo (Android package in.zavtoo.customer) and zavtoo.in for water purifier purchases, spare parts and service bookings. The app includes customer, technician and dealer access. Contact: Paanifilter9@gmail.com; account support: +91 8929029006; K-15, Raja Puri, Dwarka Road, New Delhi – 110059, India.

2Information you provide

  • Account information: name, email, mobile number, account identifier and login credentials processed by our authentication provider. Optional customer profile fields include date of birth and gender.
  • Orders and services: addresses, PIN codes, map coordinates you save, purifier details, appointment dates, service issues, booking history, purchased items, payment method/status and wallet or referral transactions.
  • Communications: support messages, uploaded photos, reviews and ratings you choose to submit. Reviews may be visible to other users.
  • Technician information: profile photo, skills, languages, experience, service areas, assigned jobs, parts inventory, earnings, payout records and verification status. Technician profile information and photos may be visible to customers. Profile photos use publicly accessible storage URLs.
  • Dealer information: shop and owner name, contact details, address, service areas, GSTIN if provided, verification status, listings, team details, orders and settlement records. Shop and product listings are visible to users.

The current app records payment methods, amounts and payment status. It does not ask for card numbers, CVV, UPI PINs or bank login passwords. Never send those details in support messages.

3Location, camera and photos

With your permission, location can be used to pin a service address and coordinate technician visits. When a technician starts a ride for an assigned job, the app can send precise live coordinates to show the technician to the assigned customer and authorized operations staff. Location sharing stops when the active ride ends or the tracking screen/app session stops. The app does not request Android background-location permission and continuous tracking while the app is closed is not supported.

Camera or photo selection is used when you choose to add RO photos, support attachments, profile photos or product pictures. We upload the images you select, rather than your entire photo library. You can deny or revoke camera and location access in Android Settings; features needing that access may then be unavailable. You can enter an address manually.

4Device information and local storage

Our hosting, authentication, database, storage and map providers receive network information such as your IP address and browser/device request information when delivering the service. The app uses device/browser storage to remember login sessions, selected account role, onboarding status and cart contents. Signing out removes the relevant login session; uninstalling or clearing app storage removes local app data but does not delete your server account.

5Why we use information

  • Create and authenticate accounts; manage customer, technician and dealer access.
  • Fulfil orders, arrange visits, share job details, provide tracking and maintain service history.
  • Manage listings, stock, verification, reviews, wallet records and settlements.
  • Answer support requests, send service-related updates, prevent misuse and meet applicable legal obligations.

We do not sell personal information. The current app does not integrate an advertising SDK or Google Analytics. Contact us to change communication preferences or request correction of your information.

6Who receives information

  • Supabase processes authentication, database records, real-time updates and uploaded files on our behalf. Website hosting providers deliver the app's web content.
  • Assigned technicians, relevant dealers and authorized Zavtoo staff receive the contact, address, service and order information needed for their work. Delivery providers receive fulfilment details when required.
  • OpenStreetMap tile servers receive network requests and the map area displayed. Opening Google Maps, WhatsApp, email or telephone links uses those services under their own privacy terms.
  • We may disclose necessary records to comply with applicable legal requests, resolve disputes or protect users and the service.

Service providers may process information outside your state or country. Access to private account records is controlled through authentication and database permissions. Public profile and listing images can be viewed by anyone with their URL. No online service can guarantee absolute security.

7Retention and deletion

We retain account information while needed to operate your account and services. You can request deletion of your Zavtoo account and associated data at zavtoo.in/delete-account without signing in, or through the Delete account link in your customer, technician or dealer profile. You can also email Paanifilter9@gmail.com with the subject 'Zavtoo account deletion'.

Deletion requests are handled by our support team. Identify the account role and registered email or phone number; we may verify ownership before acting. Do not send your password, OTP, identity documents or payment PIN. We will explain the status and expected completion time after verification. Opening an email draft alone does not submit a request: you must send it.

Deletion covers your login and associated profile, saved addresses, optional profile details, uploaded personal photos and support data that no longer need to be kept. Order, invoice, payment, warranty, fraud-prevention and dispute records may be retained where required for those purposes or by law. Public listings and technician/dealer records need review for active jobs, third-party orders and settlement obligations. We will explain any retained categories, reasons and applicable retention periods when handling your request. Backup copies may remain until the provider's normal backup cycle expires; they are not used to restore a deleted account for ordinary service use.

You may also request a copy, correction or deletion of particular personal information without closing your account by contacting the same email address. Removing the app or logging out is not an account deletion request.

8Children and policy changes

Zavtoo is intended for adult customers and business users, not children under 18. If you believe a child has provided personal information, contact us so we can investigate and remove it where appropriate.

We update this page when our data practices change and revise the date above. Material changes will be communicated through the service where appropriate. Privacy questions and requests can be sent to Paanifilter9@gmail.com or the address listed above.
