Common questions

Hiring in Montenegro — FAQ

What does it cost a Montenegrin employer to use Hirenova?
Nothing. Registration, vacancy posting, candidate search, shortlisting and interview requests are free for employers, with no posting limit and no card required.
Which countries do the workers come from?
India, Nepal, Bangladesh, Sri Lanka, Indonesia and the Philippines. Candidates are sourced and screened locally in those countries before they appear in your search results.
Are candidates checked before I see them?
Yes. Our team reviews the profile, experience certificates and identity document, and conducts a skills conversation, before a candidate becomes searchable. The screening record is shown on each candidate profile.
Can Hirenova guarantee that a selected worker will receive a work permit?
No, and neither can anyone else. Work permits and residence permits are decided by the Montenegrin authorities under the applicable law. Hirenova supports the documentation process and keeps you informed, but the decision is not ours or yours.
How long does the process take?
Sourcing and shortlisting typically move within days of a vacancy being published. The employment and immigration steps that follow depend on the authorities and on the completeness of the candidate's documents, so we give you a status rather than a promised date.
