Who we are

Recruitment support, not promises

Hirenova Workforce Pvt. Ltd. is an international workforce sourcing and recruitment-support company. We work on the sourcing side of international hiring: finding candidates in India, Nepal, Bangladesh, Sri Lanka, Indonesia and the Philippines, screening their trade experience and documents, and presenting them to employers in South-East Europe as structured, comparable profiles.

We are not an immigration authority and we do not present ourselves as one. Employment decisions belong to the employer. Visa, work-permit and residence decisions belong to the relevant government. Our job is to make everything up to that point orderly, documented and honest.

Our mission

Make international recruitment easier, transparent and professional

International recruitment has a reputation problem, earned over decades: unclear fees, exaggerated promises, workers who arrive to find the job was not what they were told. We are building the opposite — a platform where the employer sees exactly what a candidate can do, the candidate sees exactly what the job pays and provides, and both sides can see where their application stands.

Free for employers. Clearly disclosed for candidates. No guarantees about outcomes that are not ours to give.

Why Hirenova

What we actually do

International sourcing

Candidate pipelines in six countries, built around the trades European employers ask for.

Candidate screening

Experience, certificates, identity and a trade-specific skills conversation, recorded on the profile.

Employer matching

Scored matching on profession, skills, experience, availability and language — with the reasoning shown.

Interview coordination

Scheduling across time zones, with joining details and reminders for both sides.

Documentation support

Helping candidates assemble what the employer and the authorities require, correctly and on time.

Dedicated communication

A named coordinator for each employer, and an in-platform message thread that keeps a record.

Transparent process

The same status, visible to both sides, with a dated history of every step.

Data protection

Encrypted documents, role-based access, access logs the candidate can read.
