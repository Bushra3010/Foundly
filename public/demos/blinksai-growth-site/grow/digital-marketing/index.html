Risk reversal

Miss the target,get the fee back.

If the monthly lead target written into the agreement is not met within 90 days, the management fee for that period is refunded in full. The conditions are printed here rather than buried in an annexure, because every one of them changes whether the test is fair.

Minimum ad spend

₹500 a day · ₹15,000 a month

Below this the guarantee does not apply — there is not enough spend to learn from. The service itself carries on as normal.

  1. 01

    ₹500 a day in ad spend, without a break

    Continuous across all 90 days. If the budget stops midway the guarantee is void, because the test was never actually run.

  2. 02

    The lead target is fixed in writing first

    A realistic monthly number, set against your industry, city and budget, written into the agreement before the first rupee is spent.

  3. 03

    Content approved within 48 hours

    If posts and reels are not approved on time the calendar slips, and that period does not count towards the 90 days.

  4. 04

    Leads called within 24 hours

    We can deliver the leads; somebody on your side has to pick up the phone. Call logs are checked as part of any claim.

  5. 05

    The refund is the management fee, not the ad spend

    Ad spend goes to Meta and Google, not to us, so it is not ours to return. The fee you paid us is.

  6. 06

    Paid within 15 working days

    A claim triggers a joint review of the numbers, then a direct bank transfer.
