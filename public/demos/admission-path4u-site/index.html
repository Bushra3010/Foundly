JEE Main 2025

Joint Entrance Examination (JEE) Main is the gateway to admission in NITs, IIITs, and other centrally funded technical institutions. It is also the qualifying exam for JEE Advanced (IITs). The exam is conducted in two sessions — January and April.

4/15/2025
Online
10+2 or equivalent with PCM. Minimum 75% marks (65% for SC/ST). Age limit: must have been born on or after October 1, 2000.
View Details

NEET UG 2025

National Eligibility cum Entrance Test (NEET-UG) is the single entrance exam for admission to MBBS, BDS, BAMS, BSMS, BUMS, and BHMS courses in all medical colleges across India. Conducted by NTA, it is the most competitive medical entrance exam in India.

5/4/2025
Offline
10+2 with PCB (Physics, Chemistry, Biology). Minimum 50% aggregate marks (40% for SC/ST/OBC). Age: 17-25 years (relaxation for reserved categories).
View Details

CAT 2025

Common Admission Test (CAT) is India's premier MBA entrance examination, conducted by the IIMs on a rotational basis. CAT scores are accepted by all IIMs and over 1,200 B-schools across India. It tests Verbal Ability, Data Interpretation, Logical Reasoning, and Quantitative Aptitude.

11/30/2025
Online
Bachelor's degree in any discipline with minimum 50% marks (45% for SC/ST/PWD) from a recognized university. Final year students can also apply.
