Questions

Does Backseat include any channels?

No. Backseat is a player. You add your own playlists and servers, and you're responsible for having the right to watch them.

Does it work with CarPlay?

Apple doesn't allow video apps on CarPlay. Backseat sends video to car displays and adapters that support AirPlay, and Screen Mirroring in Control Center works with any app.

Why won't a channel play?

The stream may be offline or geo-blocked, or it may use a format iPhone can't decode. Try refreshing the playlist, or ask your provider for an HLS (.m3u8) link.

Why do some playlists fail in the web player?

Browsers only load playlists from servers that allow it (CORS), and secure sites can't play http:// streams. The iPhone app has neither limit. In the web player you can also upload the .m3u file instead of pasting its link.

Can I cancel Pro?

Yes. Manage or cancel it at any time in Settings › Apple Account › Subscriptions on your iPhone.
