Support

Most problems are covered below. If you're still stuck, contact the Backseat team and describe what you tried, your iPhone model and iOS version.

A playlist won't load

Check that the link starts with http:// or https:// and opens in Safari. Some providers change links regularly, so copy a fresh one. For Xtream, enter only the server address (for example http://example.com:8080) with your username and password.

A channel won't play

The stream may be offline or geo-blocked, or it may use a format iPhone can't decode, such as MPEG-2 video or DTS audio. Try another channel, refresh the playlist, or ask your provider for HLS (.m3u8) links.

Casting to the car stopped working

  1. Make sure the iPhone and the car display or adapter are on the same network.
  2. Turn the car's wireless adapter off and on, then choose it again from the AirPlay button.
  3. As a fallback, open Control Center, tap Screen Mirroring and pick the car display. This works for every app.

Restoring Backseat Pro

Open Settings in Backseat and tap Restore purchases. Use the same Apple Account you bought Pro with.

Cancelling a subscription

On your iPhone, go to Settings › your name › Subscriptions › Backseat Pro.

The web player can't open my playlist

Browsers can only read playlists from servers that allow it, and secure sites can't play http:// streams. Download the .m3u file and use Upload file in the web player, or use the iPhone app.
