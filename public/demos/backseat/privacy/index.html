Privacy policy

Last updated: 7 November 2026

Backseat is a media player for iPhone, with a companion web player. We built it so that we never see your data.

What we collect

Nothing. Backseat has no account system, no analytics and no advertising, and it does not send data to servers run by us.

What stays on your device

  • The playlists and servers you add, and the channel lists loaded from them.
  • Favourites, recently played items, watch progress and search history.
  • Passwords and access tokens for Xtream and Jellyfin/Emby servers. These are stored in the iOS Keychain.

The web player keeps the same kinds of data in your browser's local storage. Clearing your site data removes it.

Who Backseat talks to

Backseat connects only to the playlist links, streams and servers you add yourself, and to Apple's services for AirPlay, speech recognition (voice search) and in-app purchases. Voice search uses Apple's speech recognition, which may send audio to Apple to process your request.

Camera and microphone

The camera is used only to scan playlist QR codes. The microphone is used only while voice search is open. Neither is recorded or stored.

Purchases

Subscriptions and purchases are handled by Apple. We never receive your payment details.

Children

Backseat does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update this page and the date above.

Contact

Questions? See the support page.
