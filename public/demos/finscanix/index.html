The problem

Unverified rates and scope quietly drain your budget.

Eyeballing quotes and scope changes creates expensive blind spots. Rushed manual reviews allow hidden markups and unbudgeted work to pass through unchecked, turning small oversight into compounding budget leaks.

Hours lost per bill

Verifying a single item means jumping between PDFs, spreadsheets and browsers line by line. It's an exhausting, repetitive workflow that burns precious time.

Subjective approvals create compliance risks

Different team members apply different rules when evaluating quotes and rates. Without standardised data, your organisation loses control over market fairness and price accuracy.

No clear trail of evidence

Manual reviews leave behind scattered notes and untracked emails. When auditors or stakeholders ask for justification, there is no centralised record to prove how a decision was made.

Capabilities

Everything an estimator and an auditor each need.

Government SoR baseline

Seeded with CPWD DSR and State PWD schedules, versioned by effective date. Every verdict traces back to a rate code you can cite in an audit file.

Location-aware pricing

Base rates are adjusted by city cost index — Mumbai at 1.18 is not Jaipur at 0.96 — and market quotes are filtered to the project's city or PIN code.

Live market cross-check

Prices pulled from B2B and e-commerce sources with seller, platform and fetch timestamp on every quote, refreshed on a schedule so nothing goes stale.

Confidence you can see

OCR confidence per field, SoR match score per line, and a verdict confidence that tells you how much weight a flag deserves.

Audit-ready exports

Deterministic engine: the same document always produces the same report. Export to PDF or Excel with the full evidence trail intact.

Domain-restricted assistant

An assistant that answers construction, FM and engineering questions — and politely declines everything else, by design.

Your commercial data stays yours.

Vendor rates are among the most sensitive numbers a business holds. RateCheck Audit is multi-tenant with strict isolation, encrypts data in transit and at rest, and deletes exactly what you ask it to delete — one document, never a cascade.
