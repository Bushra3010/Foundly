About This Service

Getting around Kashmir's mountains and valleys requires experienced local drivers who know every road, shortcut, and seasonal route. Our cab service provides safe, comfortable, and reliable transportation across Kashmir, Ladakh, and Himachal Pradesh.

Whether you need an airport pickup, a full-day sightseeing trip, or a multi-day outstation journey — our fleet of well-maintained AC vehicles and professional drivers are at your service.

Why Choose Our Cabs?

Verified & Safe Drivers

All drivers are police-verified, licensed, and trained for mountain driving.

Well-Maintained Fleet

Clean, serviced AC vehicles — from sedans to Tempo Travellers and mini buses.

Local Knowledge

Drivers know every valley, pass, and local attraction — your personal Kashmir expert.

Groups Welcome

Vehicles for every group size — from 2 people to 32+ with Tempo Travellers and buses.

Frequently Asked Questions

Are your cabs air-conditioned?

Yes! All our vehicles are fully air-conditioned for your comfort, even on mountain routes.

Are the drivers experienced in mountain driving?

Absolutely. All our drivers have years of experience navigating Kashmir, Ladakh, and Himachal mountain roads and are familiar with local conditions.

Do you offer per-hour and per-day rates?

Yes. We offer airport transfers (per trip), local sightseeing (8 hrs / 80 km packages), and outstation trips (per day rates). Contact us for a custom quote.

What if my flight is delayed?

Our drivers track flights and will adjust pickup time accordingly at no extra charge for reasonable delays.

Can I book a cab for a full Kashmir tour?

Yes! Our tour packages include a dedicated cab and driver throughout. Alternatively, book a self-drive tour with a cab and driver for your entire trip.
