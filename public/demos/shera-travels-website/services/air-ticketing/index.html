About This Service

At Snowpeak Travels, we make flight booking simple, affordable, and stress-free. Whether you're flying to Srinagar for a Kashmir holiday, to Leh for a Ladakh adventure, or travelling internationally — our team finds you the best available fares across all major airlines.

With over a decade of experience in travel services, we have established relationships with all major domestic carriers including IndiGo, Air India, SpiceJet, GoFirst, and Vistara, as well as access to international airline inventory.

Frequently Asked Questions

Do you charge extra fees for booking?

We charge a small convenience fee which is clearly disclosed before payment. No hidden charges ever.

Can I book international flights through you?

Yes! We book both domestic and international flights. Contact us for international routes and pricing.

What if I need to cancel or reschedule?

Cancellation and reschedule policies depend on the airline's fare rules. We'll guide you through the process and help you minimize charges.

How quickly do I receive my ticket?

E-tickets are delivered instantly (within minutes) after payment confirmation, directly to your email and WhatsApp.

Do you book group flights?

Yes! We specialize in group bookings (10+ passengers) and often get better negotiated rates for groups.
