NEET PG counselling — FAQs

Which PG seats does MCC counselling cover?+

MCC conducts counselling for 50% All India Quota MD/MS/Diploma seats in government colleges, 100% seats of Deemed and Central Universities (including DU, BHU, AMU internal quotas) and DNB / DNB Diploma seats of NBEMS-accredited hospitals.

Where does the PG cut-off data come from?+

All closing ranks are taken from MCC's official PG allotment results for 2023, 2024 and 2025 (Round 1, 2, 3 and stray). Each row shows the opening and closing rank for a college, degree, branch, quota and category.

What is the difference between MD/MS and DNB seats?+

MD/MS seats are in NMC-recognised medical colleges; DNB seats are run by NBEMS in accredited hospitals. Both are recognised postgraduate qualifications, and DNB seats often close at higher ranks in the same branch.

How should I balance branch vs college?+

Most candidates rank branches first and then colleges, but a strong government college in a slightly less preferred branch can be a better option than a weak private seat. Our choice filling advisor shows both views.

Are state quota PG cut-offs included?+

Not yet — MCC publishes only All India, Deemed, Central and DNB results. For state quota our predictor shows an estimate; our counsellors share state-specific data on call.
