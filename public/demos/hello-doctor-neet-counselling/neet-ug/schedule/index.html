NEET UG 2026 — MCC AIQ Counselling Schedule (MBBS, BDS & B.Sc Nursing)

Round 1

  1. Final allotment resultPublished 24 Aug 2026

Round 2

  1. Seat matrix verification1–2 Sep 2026
  2. Registration / payment3–8 Sep 2026 (till 3 PM)
  3. Choice filling / locking3–9 Sep 2026 (till 10 AM); locking from 4 PM, 8 Sep
  4. Seat allotment processing9–10 Sep 2026
  5. Result11 Sep 2026
  6. Reporting12–18 Sep 2026

Round 3

  1. Seat matrix verification21 Sep 2026
  2. Registration / payment22–27 Sep 2026 (till 3 PM)
  3. Choice filling / locking23–28 Sep 2026 (till 12 noon); locking from 7 PM, 27 Sep
  4. Seat allotment processing28–29 Sep 2026
  5. Result30 Sep 2026
  6. Reporting1–9 Oct 2026

Stray Vacancy Round

  1. Registration / payment12–14 Oct 2026 (till 3 PM)
  2. Choice filling / locking12–15 Oct 2026 (till 10 AM); locking from 4 PM, 14 Oct
  3. Seat allotment processing15–16 Oct 2026
  4. Result17 Oct 2026
  5. Reporting19–26 Oct 2026
