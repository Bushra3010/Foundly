FAQs

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the difference between Trust, Society, and Section 8 Company?+
All three are NGO structures in India. Society is registered under Societies Registration Act (needs 7+ members). Trust is under Indian Trusts Act (needs 2+ trustees). Section 8 Company is under Companies Act 2013 — most credible, MCA regulated, with highest governance standards.
Can I register an NGO online without visiting any government office?+
Yes! With LegalEase Online, the entire process is online. We handle all government portals, document submission, and follow-ups. You just need to upload your documents via our secure portal.
What documents are needed for NGO registration?+
Basic documents: Aadhar card and PAN of all members/trustees, address proof, passport photos, registered office address proof, and email/phone of key persons. Specific requirements vary by NGO type.
How much does NGO registration cost at LegalEase Online?+
NGO registration starts at ₹4,999 for Trust/Society. Section 8 Company starts at ₹6,999. All prices include government fees, professional charges, and certificate delivery. No hidden charges.
What is the timeline for FCRA registration?+
FCRA applications are processed by MHA and typically take 3–6 months. The organization must be registered for minimum 3 years and have spent ₹15+ lakhs on activities.
How long does trademark registration take?+
After filing, you can use the ™ symbol immediately. Full ® registration takes 12-24 months subject to objections and oppositions. We keep you updated throughout the process.
