FAQs for Mumbai Clients

What are the requirements for NGO registration in Mumbai?+
NGO registration in Mumbai, Maharashtra follows standard central government requirements with some state-specific variations. You need minimum members (7 for Society, 2 for Trust), address proof in Maharashtra, and relevant KYC documents. We guide you through all Maharashtra-specific requirements.
How long does NGO registration take in Mumbai?+
With LegalEase Online, NGO registration in Mumbai typically takes 7-10 working days after complete document submission. Section 8 Company takes 15-20 days due to MCA processing. We ensure fast processing with no unnecessary delays.
Is it necessary to have an office in Mumbai for registration?+
Yes, you need a registered office address in Mumbai/Maharashtra for local NGO registration. This can be a home address initially. We can guide you on the specific address requirements for your registration type.
