Frequently Asked Questions

What is GSTR-3B?+
GSTR-3B is a monthly self-declaration return that summarizes outward and inward supplies, ITC claimed, and tax paid.
What happens if GST return is filed late?+
Late filing attracts interest at 18% per annum on tax due, plus late fees of ₹50/day (₹25/day for nil return).
