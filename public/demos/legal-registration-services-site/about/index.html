Our Story

Founded on a Mission to Make Legal Services Accessible

LegalEase Online was founded on a simple mission — to make India's complex legal registration system accessible, transparent, and affordable for everyone, from NGO founders in small towns to entrepreneurs launching startups in metros.

Starting in 2009 with a focus on NGO registration services in Jharkhand, LegalEase Online quickly expanded as clients experienced the difference — clear pricing, no hidden charges, and genuine legal expertise delivered by registered advocates.

Over 15 years, LegalEase Online has evolved into India's leading digital legal consultancy, serving 5,000+ clients across all major Indian cities. Our government trademark registration and ISO certification reflect our commitment to quality and authenticity.

Today, our team of Bar Council-registered advocates, paralegals, and compliance experts handles thousands of applications annually — from NGO registrations in remote Jharkhand villages to Section 8 Company incorporations for Mumbai-based corporates.
